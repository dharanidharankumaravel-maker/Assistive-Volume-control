## 📝 Full Description (Google Play Store Formatted)

```text
VolumePilot is a private, lightweight assistive volume controller designed for users with broken hardware volume buttons, motor impairments, or a preference for fast one-handed audio control.

Unlike generic "volume booster" apps that risk speaker hardware damage or flood your device with intrusive ads, VolumePilot is an ethical, privacy-first utility: zero ads, zero tracking, zero accounts, and strictly ZERO internet permission.

🎚️ RELIABLE FLOATING ASSISTANT
• Always Accessible: A sleek floating volume bubble rests unobtrusively on your screen edge over any app, game, or video.
• One-Handed Control: Easily adjust media, ringtones, notifications, and alarms without straining to reach physical hardware buttons.
• Magnetic Edge Snapping: Drags smoothly and automatically docks to either screen edge to stay out of your content.
• Gesture Shortcuts: Double-tap to quick-mute; long-press to cycle active audio streams.
• Never Disappears: Survives app switches, orientation changes, and device reboot (optional Start on Boot).

🔊 INTELLIGENT PROFILES & PRESETS
• Instant Sound Setups: One-tap switching between Default, Sleep, Work, Driving, Meeting, Gaming, and Headphones modes.
• Custom Profiles: Create and save your own multi-stream volume levels and ringer mode combinations (Normal, Vibrate, Silent).
• Output Detection: Automatically detects connected audio output devices (Speaker, Bluetooth, Wired Headphones, USB Audio).

⚡ BATTERY-EFFICIENT AUTOMATION
• Device Rules: Automatically switch to your Driving profile when connecting to your car's Bluetooth, or Headphones profile when plugging in a headset.
• Time-Scheduled Rules: Automatically silence notifications during bedtime or reduce volume during work hours.
• Zero CPU Polling: Pure event-driven Kotlin Flow architecture aligned with Android Doze mode. No battery drain, no CPU wake locks.

🛡️ SAFE LISTENING & HEARING HEALTH
• Max Volume Limiter: Set a safe decibel ceiling (e.g., 80%) on media and Bluetooth audio to protect your hearing against unexpected loud sounds.
• Emergency Stream Protection: Alarm and In-Call volumes are never suppressed, ensuring you never miss important calls or morning alarms.

🔒 100% PRIVACY & OFFLINE RELIABILITY
• No Internet Permission: VolumePilot does not request android.permission.INTERNET. Not a single byte of data can leave your device.
• Zero Tracking & Zero SDKs: No Google Analytics, no Firebase, no advertising trackers, and no remote telemetry.
• Local Data Backup: Export and import your custom profiles and automation rules anytime as plain text JSON.
• Built-In Health Diagnostics: Real-time permission monitor and self-healing overlay repair with OEM battery optimization tips.

♿ ACCESSIBILITY FIRST (WCAG 2.1 AA)
• Full TalkBack screen reader support with custom accessibility actions.
• Stepped +/- buttons alongside continuous sliders for precision control without dragging.
• Guaranteed 48dp+ minimum touch targets on all interactive buttons.
• High contrast styling and dynamic font scale support up to 200%.

Give your physical volume buttons a rest. Download VolumePilot today for private, reliable, and effortless sound control.
```
