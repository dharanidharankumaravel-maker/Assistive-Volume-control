1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & CORE PROMISE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VolumePilot (com.volumepilot) is a production-grade, privacy-first, battery-efficient
Android application designed for users with broken hardware volume buttons, motor
accessibility needs, or single-handed mobility requirements.

Non-Negotiable Principles Adhered To:
- Reliability First: Persistent floating overlay survives app switches, orientation
  changes, and device reboot (Start on Boot).
- Privacy by Default: Zero ads, zero accounts, zero analytics SDKs, zero remote tracking,
  and strictly ZERO android.permission.INTERNET in the manifest or dependencies.
- Battery Efficiency: Pure event-driven architecture using Kotlin Flows, AudioManager
  callbacks, and Doze-aligned AlarmManager. Zero polling loops, zero CPU wake locks.
- Accessibility First: Full WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance, 48dp+ touch targets, TalkBack
  custom accessibility actions, dynamic font scaling up to 200%.
- Anti-Deception: Zero misleading "volume booster" claims; includes a configurable
  decibel ceiling limiter (EnforceMaxVolumeCapUseCase) to protect hearing health.

PHASE 2: Core Volume Engine & Reactive Audio Layer
- VolumeController interface & AndroidVolumeController implementation.
- Reactive observation of audio state via callbackFlow, listening to AudioManager
  broadcasts and AudioDeviceCallback.
- Granular control of streams (MEDIA, RING, NOTIFICATION, ALARM, VOICE_CALL).
- RingerMode toggling (NORMAL, VIBRATE, SILENT).
- Safe listening decibel ceiling limiter (EnforceMaxVolumeCapUseCase) protecting MEDIA
  and BLUETOOTH while leaving ALARM and VOICE_CALL unconstrained for emergency safety.

PHASE 3: Floating Assistive Overlay & Lifecycle Host
- OverlayService declared as foregroundServiceType="specialUse" with property
  PROPERTY_SPECIAL_USE_FGS_SUBTYPE per Android 14+ (API 34+) requirements.
- OverlayLifecycleOwner attaching LifecycleOwner, SavedStateRegistryOwner, and
  ViewModelStoreOwner to ComposeView in WindowManager (TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY).
- OverlayBubbleContent with delta touch tracking, touch slop, and magnetic edge snapping.
- OverlayPanelContent with stream tabs, responsive sliders, quick mute, and stepped +/-.
- BootReceiver restoring overlay on BOOT_COMPLETED when enabled.

PHASE 4: Permissions, Onboarding & Reliability
- OnboardingScreen with plain-language permission checklist (Overlay, Notifications).
- DiagnosticsScreen: Real-time health telemetry monitor displaying permission state,

- Time-scheduled automation: Battery-efficient AlarmManager inexact repeats
